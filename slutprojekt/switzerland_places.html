Top Places in Switzerland

Lake Geneva

Location: Genèvesjön, Switzerland

A massive, crescent-shaped glacial lake serving as the largest lake in Western Europe along the Rhône River.

Lake Lungern

Location: Lungerersee, Switzerland

A natural lake in Obwalden, Switzerland which is named after the town Lungern on its shore. The lake is drained by the Sarner Aa river.
